FAQ

Quick answers.

Is my journal data state-compliant if it lives in your app?

Most states accept electronic journals as long as the entries contain the required fields and are tamper-evident. We follow the strictest state's requirements (California) by default and provide audit-ready PDF exports any time you need them. We're not lawyers — always verify with your state's notary commission, but we've designed Wayseal to satisfy all 50 states' digital journal rules.

What if SnapDocs blocks email forwarding?

Wayseal ingests from any source — SnapDocs notifications, NotaryDash, direct emails from title companies, even raw text you paste in. We're not relying on any one platform.

I have years of paper journals. Can you really digitize them?

Yes — try the free paper-journal decoder right now. Snap a photo of any page; we extract every row in 10 seconds. Pro plan includes unlimited photo imports.

How accurate is the AI on handwritten entries?

We use Gemini 2.0 multimodal vision. Typed entries are ~98% accurate; handwritten entries are 80–92% depending on legibility. Every parsed entry is reviewable and editable before it commits to your journal — we never auto-save anything you can't verify.
